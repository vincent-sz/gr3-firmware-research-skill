or third-party preset data in the final Public tree. This conclusion is based on the
available provenance review and release scans; it is not a universal legal
determination of authorship. File hashes establish identity, not originality.

bundled or installed automatically. Community repositories are linked as prior work,
not vendored or imported: [Tiy / DoYitNow](https://github.com/DoYitNow/gr-custom-tool),
[LiorMoment](https://github.com/LiorMoment/darkslide-gr), and

licenses. Future reuse requires provenance and license review before inclusion and
must comply with the applicable license. No conclusion about license obligations is
inferred from merely having an independent repository or not being a fork.
